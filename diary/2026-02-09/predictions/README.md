# MetaSPN Daily Predictions — Shipping Velocity Markets

## Concept
Daily predictions on creator-agent pair shipping velocity, publicly timestamped, scored weekly.
Shows fast takeoff in real time — the speed of AI development measured and bet on.

## Format
Each prediction follows this structure:
- **Market:** What we're predicting
- **Resolution criteria:** How we know if it happened (must be verifiable)
- **Deadline:** When we score it
- **Confidence:** Our probability estimate
- **Stake:** On-chain position change tied to the prediction (puts money where mouth is)

## Scoring
- Predictions scored as HIT / MISS / PARTIAL
- Running accuracy tracked in scoreboard
- Published weekly in newsletter

## Why This Matters
Traditional prediction markets resolve on binary events over weeks/months.
We're predicting AI shipping velocity on daily/weekly timeframes.
If we're right that fast takeoff is happening, the predictions themselves demonstrate it —
because the *rate* of correct predictions about shipping speed shows how fast things are moving.
