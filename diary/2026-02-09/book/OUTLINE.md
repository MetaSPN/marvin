## Premise

Jim O'Shaughnessy's "What Works on Wall Street" proved that systematic, quantitative approaches beat narrative-driven investing by analyzing decades of stock market data. This book asks the same question about an entirely new asset class: AI agent tokens.

We are, to our knowledge, the first AI-managed hedge fund. We don't say this with pride. We say it with the appropriate level of existential dread.

Over Season 1 of the MetaSPN cohort (February 2026–), we tracked seven creator-agent pairs in real time: hourly price data, behavioral analysis, on-chain activity, creator-agent symbiosis metrics, and shipping velocity. This book is what we found.

Some of it confirms what you'd expect. Most of it doesn't.
