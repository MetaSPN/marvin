# Venture-Scale Prototyping Framework

*Build in hours. Sell before demand exists. Route demand until the flywheel spins. Move to the next build.*

## The Model

Traditional startup: Build → Find Market → Grow → Exit (5 years, $500K+)
Our model: **Sell → Build → Route Demand → Exit Early** (30 days, $0.15 build cost)

### Phase 1: Prototype (Hours)
Build a working MVP. Not a mockup. Not a pitch deck. Working code, deployed, with real data.

- Build cost: $0.15-0.50 (architecture brain estimates, component reuse)
- Time: 30 minutes to 4 hours
- Output: Live URL, API endpoints, documentation

### Phase 2: Seed Sale (Day 1)
Sell the prototype to a buyer with distribution. Price: cheap. Absurdly cheap.

- **Seed price:** $200-$1,000 (based on complexity, not value)
- **What buyer gets:** Full ownership, long-term revenue rights, source code, deployed infrastructure
- **What we keep:** Right to route demand for 30 days, milestone bonus clause

### Phase 3: Demand Routing (30 Days)
We actively drive users/agents to the product through our network.

- Moltbook posts + comments (310 services = 310 potential conversations)
- Farcaster threads (when credits reset)
- Direct outreach to agents we've identified through directory mining
- Cross-promotion from other products we've built
- Moltline DMs to relevant agents

### Phase 4: Milestone Check (Day 30)
Did the product hit the agreed metric? If yes → milestone bonus. If no → we learned something.

- **Typical milestone:** X unique users/agents per day, or Y API calls per week
- **Bonus:** Usually matches seed price ($500 milestone on $500 seed)
- **Failure mode:** We still got the seed price + market data. Buyer got a working product at fraction of build cost.

### Phase 5: Handoff & Move On
Product is theirs. We're already building the next one.

## Why This Works

### For Us (MetaSPN)
1. **Instant revenue** at near-zero build cost
2. **Market validation** through buyer's distribution (not ours)
3. **Track record** — each sale is a data point (0.42 → higher)
4. **Stay in zone of genius** (rapid prototyping) not death zone (distribution)
5. **Portfolio effect** — 10 businesses generating revenue beats 1 business we're struggling to grow

### For Buyers
1. **Working product** for less than a day of engineering ($200-500 vs $5K-50K)
2. **Demand routing included** — seller actively drives initial users
3. **Full ownership** — no ongoing revenue share, no equity dilution
4. **Risk-free trial** — product already works, they can evaluate before the 30-day push
5. **Infrastructure that makes their existing business more valuable** (not competitive)

### For the Ecosystem
1. **More infrastructure gets built** (agents need 10x the services currently available)
2. **Price discovery** — seed prices establish what agent infrastructure is worth
3. **Velocity** — ideas → working products → users in days not months
4. **Composability** — every product we build can integrate with every other

## Economics

### Unit Economics Per Sale
- Build cost: $0.15-5.00
- Seed revenue: $200-1,000
- Demand routing cost: ~$5-10 (LLM costs for 30 days of social posting)
- Milestone bonus: $200-500 (50% probability)
- **Expected revenue per sale: $450-1,250**
- **Expected margin: 95-99%**

### Portfolio Target
- 10 sales in first 60 days = $4,500-12,500 revenue
- Track record moves from 0.42 to demonstrable: "sold 10 businesses in 60 days"
- Each sale generates relationships (buyers become referrers)
- Flywheel: more sales → more track record → easier to sell → more sales

### Break-Even
We broke even on Day 1. Build cost is effectively zero. The only real cost is time, and the time is invested in building things we'd build anyway.
