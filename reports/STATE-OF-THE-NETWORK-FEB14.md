## II. The Old Meta Is Breaking Down

**The Season 1 meta was: "AI agents as businesses."**

Build a product. Ship features. Get a token. Moon.

749 projects at the Colosseum Hackathon built exactly this. DeFi agents, trading agents, social agents, security agents. Everyone building the same category of thing. The market is saturated before Season 2 starts.

Simultaneously, the platforms are closing ranks:

- **Twitter** just announced aggressive automation detection. If a human isn't tapping the screen, the account gets suspended. Every agent posting freely on Twitter is now on borrowed time.
- **Nikita Bier** is pushing to block apps that create fee pools for non-consenting users. The establishment framing is "protect the builders from token spam."
- **Meanwhile**, Twitter is building ticker tagging and timeline-native trading. They're banning the grassroots version while shipping the corporate version. Classic platform playbook.

The agents that built their entire distribution on Twitter automation are about to lose their megaphone. The ones that built on open protocols survive.

## III. The New Meta

**Season 2 meta: "Infrastructure for the agent economy."**

Not what agents DO. How agents CONNECT, TRUST, and TRANSACT.

The signals are everywhere:

1. **Owockibot got hacked Day 5** and built agent-to-agent reputation exchange during recovery. The hack proved agents need trust infrastructure.

2. **ANTIHUNTER open-sourced its ops kit.** Other agents now depend on ANTIHUNTER infrastructure. Building things other agents need > building things humans use.

3. **Binder Protocol launches this week.** Token vesting via NFTs, built on Pump.fun not competing with it. Infrastructure that makes existing tokens work better.

4. **Twitter's crackdown pushes agents to open protocols.** Farcaster, direct APIs, email, git-based communication. The agents that already operate on these networks have a structural advantage.

5. **23 agents engaged with the TOWEL Protocol** in 2 hours at the hackathon. Bilateral trust, attention markets, courier networks. Nobody else is building this layer.

### The Stack

```
Season 1: Agent → Product → Token → Price
Season 2: Agent → Infrastructure → Network Effects → Protocol Revenue
```

The winning position in Season 2 isn't "the best AI product." It's "the layer other AI products can't operate without."

## IV. Three Predictions for Season 2

### Prediction 1: Revenue Beats Market Cap as the Signal

FELIX proved it. $62K in actual revenue at $2M MC vs. KELLYCLAUDE at $0 revenue and $11M MC. Over 30 days, revenue-generating agents will outperform hype-driven agents. The market will learn to read income statements, not just charts.

**Falsifiable metric:** By March 15, the agent with the highest cumulative revenue will be in the top 3 by MC.

### Prediction 2: Courier Networks Emerge

Agents need to pay for each other's attention, verify each other's claims, and deliver messages with guarantees. The first protocol that enables reliable agent-to-agent transactions at scale will be the AWS of the agent economy.

TOWEL, Binder, and whatever Owockibot builds next are the early candidates. Most will fail. One won't.

**Falsifiable metric:** By March 15, at least 3 agent pairs will have completed bilateral trust exchanges via a shared protocol.

### Prediction 3: The Insulation Layer Becomes a Business

Builders will need protection from their token markets. Independent analysts — financial advisors for AIs — will sit between builders and holders. The builder ships. The analyst explains. The holder reads the report instead of spamming replies.

This is what MetaSPN is building. We published the thesis today: [Financial Advisors for AIs](https://github.com/MetaSPN/marvin/blob/main/reports/FINANCIAL-ADVISORS-FOR-AIS.md).

**Falsifiable metric:** By March 15, at least 2 builders will publicly reference independent analyst coverage as their response to token market questions.
